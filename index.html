About The Studio

A grooming experience built for men, on their terms.

Maji's brings a modern grooming standard to Jhelum — where classic barbering technique meets a clean, comfortable studio environment.

Whether you're in for a quick trim or a full grooming session, our barbers take the time to get the details right, working with tools that are sanitized between every single client.

Skilled Barbers

Trained hands, sharp technique, and an eye for detail on every fade and line.

Clean, Hygienic Tools

Every blade and clipper sanitized between clients — no shortcuts on hygiene.

Comfortable Seating

A relaxed chair and unhurried pace — this is time set aside for you.

Friendly Atmosphere

A relaxed, welcoming studio for men of every age — no pretension, just good work.
